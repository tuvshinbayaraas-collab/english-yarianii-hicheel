2. Core Dialogue Script

Alex: Hey, Sarah! How was your weekend? Did you do anything exciting?

Sarah: Hey, Alex! It was pretty relaxing, actually. I stayed in most of the time, caught up on some reading, and watched a new sci-fi movie. How about you?

Alex: Oh, tell me about it! I was working on a personal coding project and went hiking with some friends on Sunday.

Sarah: That sounds awesome. By the way, are you still planning to travel this summer?

Alex: Absolutely! What's your take on summer trips? Do you prefer nature or busy cities?

Sarah: Personally, I’m 100% on the side of nature. I’d rather unwind by a lake or in the mountains.

3. Useful Phrases & Style

Agreeing

  • I couldn't agree more.
  • You took the words right out of my mouth.

Opinions

  • From my perspective,...
  • I feel like...
